How I became an engineer in Simple Rockets 2.

18 Oct 2026programminggame

Simple Rockets 2 is where I learned to think like an engineer.

ABOUT THE GAME

Simple Rockets 2

A spaceflight sandbox where you design rockets, planes and stations part by part, then fly them.

VISIT SIMPLEROCKETS.COM ↗

Game by Jundroo · simplerockets.com. Illustrations here are my own, drawn as inline SVG.

In this noteThe aerodynamic rocketThe space stationChip programsOne of the fastest planesVTOL rocket

01The aerodynamic rocket

I build rockets and planes and keep redesigning them until they fly well. One of my best builds is a rocket with good aerodynamics: a clean shape that cuts through the air instead of fighting it.

02The space station

I also built a huge space station with many docking ports, and I put astronauts on it. Many docks means many ships can visit at once.

03Chip programs

My favorite part is programming the embedded chip inside the game. It's so much fun. My programs run autonomously: they handle staging, pitch and deploying the payload, so the rocket flies its mission without me.

I like building things that run on their own.

04One of the fastest planes

I also made one of the fastest planes in the game. I added a rocket to it, used the tinker panel to keep the weight light, and wrote a small program that makes the rocket push the plane with unlimited fuel.

05VTOL rocket

I built a VTOL rocket too. Same habit as on the web: build small, test, fix, repeat.

Want to build your own? Start at simplerockets.com.
